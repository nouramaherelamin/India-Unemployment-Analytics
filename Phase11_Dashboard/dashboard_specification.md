# Phase 11 Dashboard Specification

## Audience
Portfolio / academic data-analysis presentation.

## Main narrative
National COVID unemployment shock -> regional heterogeneity -> anomaly concentration -> statistical evidence -> forecasting limitations.

## Required visual hierarchy
1. KPI row
2. National trend
3. Period comparison
4. Rural vs Urban
5. Regional shock
6. Baseline vs shock
7. Anomalies
8. Clusters
9. Forecast benchmark
10. Statistical evidence
11. Limitations

## Guardrails
- Keep observed and forecasted values visually distinct.
- Do not imply causality from correlation.
- Do not rank regions as "best" or "worst".
- Flag incomplete regional coverage.
- State that clustering is descriptive and small-n.
- State that long-horizon forecasting is unsupported.
